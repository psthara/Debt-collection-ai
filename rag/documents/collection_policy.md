# Collection Policy

Customer communications must be respectful, accurate, and consistent with approved lender policy.

Agents must use the approved authentication process before disclosing account-specific information.

Agents may discuss repayment arrangements only within their approved authority.

A promise-to-pay should record the promised amount and date in the approved collection system.

If a customer indicates financial hardship, follow the approved hardship-assessment process.

Exceptions outside agent authority must be escalated to an authorized human reviewer.
